Ranking Logic

Why isn't the biggest CO₂ saving always ranked first?

Carbon Core considers whether an intervention is practical, affordable and sufficiently supported by the available evidence — not just its theoretical carbon saving.

Carbon Impact × Circular Value × Feasibility × Confidence ÷ Cost + Disruption
Responsible Recommendation

Carbon numbers and AI are kept separate.

Carbon calculations remain based on physical activity and configured emission factors. AI is used for interpretation, intervention matching and ranking.
