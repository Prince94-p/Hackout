Carbon Core

Industrial emission leak-point detection, circular alternatives and decarbonization intelligence for SMEs.

01

Introduction

Carbon Core ("we", "our", "us") is an industrial emission leak-point detector and circular alternative recommendation platform built by Team TechVortex for HackOut'26.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, processed, stored and protected when you use Carbon Core or a locally hosted instance of the application.

✓

Carbon Core is designed around industrial and factory-level operational information rather than consumer profiling. We collect only the data required to provide emission analysis and decarbonization recommendations.

02

Information we collect

Account Information

  • Full name and email address used for login and workspace identification
  • Organization name
  • Securely hashed account password

Factory & Operational Data

  • Factory name, industry, location and reporting period
  • Energy sources and annual consumption including electricity and fuel usage
  • Material inputs, quantities and recycled-content percentages
  • Waste streams, waste categories and disposal methods
  • Compressed-air information such as pressure, leakage and energy consumption
  • Process-level manufacturing and operational data

Calculated & Generated Data

  • Emission baselines expressed in tCO₂e
  • Emission hotspot detection results
  • Root-cause diagnostics
  • Circular economy recommendations and confidence scores
  • Saved what-if simulation scenarios
  • Decarbonization actions, priorities and roadmap sequencing

Technical Data

  • Browser type and browser version
  • Access timestamps
  • Authentication tokens used to maintain your application session
03

How we use your information

  • Authenticate users and provide secure workspace access
  • Calculate emission baselines from factory activity data
  • Detect major emission hotspots
  • Identify potential root causes behind high emissions
  • Generate circular economy interventions
  • Run emission and cost what-if simulations
  • Build prioritized decarbonization roadmaps
  • Maintain application reliability and performance
✦

Carbon Core does not use factory information for advertising, behavioural profiling or unrelated commercial purposes.

04

Data storage & security

Carbon Core is designed to minimize unnecessary movement of industrial operational data.

Local Database Application data can be stored in a local SQLite database on the deployed server.
Password Security Passwords are hashed using bcrypt rather than stored in plaintext.
Authentication Signed JWT tokens with expiration are used for authenticated sessions.
Secure Transport HTTPS is recommended for production deployment and secure communication.

Factory information is not intentionally transmitted to third-party advertising or analytics services as part of Carbon Core's core analysis workflow.

05

Data sharing

We do not sell or rent factory operational data. Emission information, simulation results and generated recommendations remain within the Carbon Core workspace and deployment environment.

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law, regulation, court order or valid legal process.

06

Your rights

  • Access: View factory information through your Carbon Core workspace.
  • Correction: Update factory and account information through the application.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated factory information.
  • Export: Access generated emission summaries, recommendations and roadmap information.
07

Cookies & local storage

Carbon Core does not rely on tracking cookies. Browser localStorage may be used to maintain essential application state.

  • Authentication token for session persistence
  • Active factory identifier
  • Temporary simulation and scenario data

Stored browser data can be removed by logging out or clearing your browser's local storage.

08

Demo data

Carbon Core may include a pre-loaded demonstration factory such as "Apex Components Pvt. Ltd." containing synthetic operational information.

Demo information is fictional and is intended solely to demonstrate the platform's analytical capabilities.

09

Children's privacy

Carbon Core is an industrial sustainability and operational analysis platform and is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

10

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Carbon Core evolves. Material revisions will be reflected on this page together with a revised update date.

11
