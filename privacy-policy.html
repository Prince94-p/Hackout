Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Carbon Core ("we", "our", "us") is an industrial emission leak-point detector and circular alternative recommender built for the HackOut'26 hackathon by Team TechVortex. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our web application at carboncore.app or any locally hosted instance.

Carbon Core is designed to process factory-level operational data — not personal consumer data. We collect only what is necessary to deliver emission analysis and decarbonization recommendations.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Full name and email address (used for login and workspace identification)
  • Organization name
  • Hashed password (we never store plaintext passwords)

Factory & Operational Data

  • Factory name, industry, location, and reporting period
  • Energy sources and annual consumption (kWh, fuel types)
  • Material inputs, recycled content percentages, and quantities
  • Waste streams, types, and disposal methods
  • Compressed air system data (pressure, leakage, energy)
  • Process-level information and selected manufacturing operations

Calculated & Generated Data

  • Emission baselines (tCO₂e) computed from your activity data
  • Hotspot detection results and root-cause diagnostics
  • Circular economy recommendations and confidence scores
  • What-if simulation scenarios you create and save
  • Decarbonization roadmap actions and sequencing

Technical Data

  • Browser type and version
  • Access timestamps
  • Authentication tokens (JWT, stored in your browser's localStorage)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and provide workspace access
  • To calculate emission baselines from your factory activity data
  • To detect emission hotspots and identify root causes
  • To generate circular economy recommendations
  • To run what-if simulations on your emission scenarios
  • To build prioritized decarbonization roadmaps
  • To maintain and improve application performance
We do not use your factory data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering Carbon Core's analysis and recommendations to you.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored in a local SQLite database on the server where Carbon Core is deployed
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt before storage
  • Authentication uses signed JWT tokens with expiration
  • No factory data is transmitted to third-party services or external APIs
  • All communication between your browser and the server uses the connection protocol of the host (HTTPS recommended for production)

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your factory data with any third party. Your emission data, simulation results, and recommendations remain within your Carbon Core workspace.

The only exception is if required by law or legal process.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your factory data through the Carbon Core dashboard at any time.
  • Correction: You can update your factory data and account information through the application.
  • Deletion: You may request full deletion of your account and all associated factory data by contacting us.
  • Export: Your emission summary, recommendations, and roadmap data are accessible through the application interface.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

Carbon Core does not use tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage to store:

  • Your authentication token (for session persistence)
  • Active factory ID (for workspace state)
  • Temporary simulation/scenario data (for cross-page navigation)

You can clear this data at any time by logging out or clearing your browser's local storage.

8. Demo Data

Carbon Core includes a built-in demo factory ("Apex Components Pvt. Ltd.") pre-loaded with synthetic operational data. This demo data is entirely fictional and does not represent any real company or facility.

9. Children's Privacy

Carbon Core is an industrial analysis tool not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Carbon Core evolves. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the application after changes constitutes acceptance.
